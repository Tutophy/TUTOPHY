**CYBER BUREAU OF INTELLIGENCE**

*An Independent Cyber Intelligence Initiative*

The Cyber Bureau of Intelligence (CBI) represents an independent professional identity focused on understanding the digital world through technical research, cybersecurity, network intelligence and systematic investigation.

Its philosophy is built around a simple principle:

> Intelligence begins with observation. Security begins with understanding.

CBI is a personal research and technology identity, not a government agency or law enforcement organization.
